How these stats are calculated
  • Record and win rate use every graded pick. Win rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses); pushes are left out.
  • Units follow the KP system: favourites risk enough to win 1U (−180 risks 1.80U), underdogs risk 1U. A stated stake (e.g. 5U) overrides that. Gaby is tracked at a flat 1U risked on every play (or the stake she stated); her few plays posted without a price use −110. Parlays are priced from the combined odds of every leg, so a stack of −1000 legs risks a lot to win 1U.
  • Prices, in order: odds posted with the pick; otherwise the closing line at the exact same number (FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM first; closing prices marked °); PrizePicks-style picks at −137 a leg.
  • Picks with no price at all are estimated at a flat 1U risked at −110 a leg (a 2-leg parlay at +264). Their odds are marked * in the database. WhizBang is left out of this: his unpriced plays are mostly heavy-chalk legs, and −110 would credit him units that never existed, so his unpriced picks count in the record only until real prices are loaded.
  • Grades come from the official box scores and are never changed once settled.
